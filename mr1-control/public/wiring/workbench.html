MR1 / CL57T V4.1 / Wiring workbench · 24 September 2026

Start with the unpowered build.

This is a preparation and inspection worksheet, not a released electrical schematic. The interface, connector faces, protection ratings and safety circuit still need physical verification. Nothing here authorizes energizing or machining.

Useful work to do now

  1. Keep mains, spindle, DC supplies and USB disconnected; establish and verify the de-energized condition before continuity work. Hazardous-energy work needs an appropriately qualified person.
  2. Record the board revision and MCU, each drive revision, motor labels and every connector face. Preserve the stock wiring and settings backup.
  3. Label both ends of each drive, motor and encoder set X, YL, Z or YR. Keep matched phase and encoder harnesses together.
  4. Dry-fit retained, keyed adapters and route cables with strain relief. Separate motor/bus wiring from encoder and low-voltage signal wiring. Verify reach before cutting or drilling.
  5. Trace and record each proposed connection in the blank circuit ledger. Leave unknown interface terminals, fuse sizes, wire ratings and physical cavity numbers blank and on HOLD.

Do not guess these five things

  • Connector orientation: a schematic contact number is not a count from the top of a rotated board or a view into a mating plug.
  • Voltage domains: P4 is polarized motion power; P1 uses the qualified 5 V command interface; P2 VCC is the drive's encoder-supply output.
  • Module identity: “TLP281” or “HW-399” does not establish the board circuit. An unpowered common pull-up rail can still couple channels.
  • Fault protection: PB1 needs the combined conditioned healthy signals. PG12–PG15 are diagnostic indications. Raw ALM/COMO outputs are not assumed dry contacts.
  • Power and spindle: the recorded 36 V/10 A supply has not been accepted for simultaneous loading; spindle scaling and its field interface remain unresolved.

The two Y drives move one gantry. A logical Y jog commands both outputs; it does not independently verify either motor. Actual directions and coupler fit are still unproven.

Driver-socket references: STEP = schematic contact 7, DIR = 8, logic GND = 9; EN = 1 is reserved. The Octopus v1.1 schematic uses 5 V buffers on these socket signals. Confirm the actual footprint view and keying before mating an adapter. No plug-in stepper driver or motor phase wire belongs in this command path.

Expected conditioned input behavior

Home inputs and the PB1 fault aggregate must be healthy-low and become high on the specified trigger/fault, open cable or lost field power. The active-low probe and setter need their own proven input truth tables. These are circuit requirements, not a claim that a generic isolation module achieves them.

The app's Read-only wiring diagnostics displays decoded logical state and can export labelled observations while disarmed. It does not measure volts, certify an E-stop, distinguish both Y limit channels, show individual PG12–PG15 drive states, or show the unselected probe.

FAN and heater output minus terminals are switched power returns, not signal ground. FAN positive voltage depends on the actual selector. Do not attach spindle or coolant field wiring from GPIO names alone.

The cabinet's independent safety circuit removes hazardous-energy permission. The Octopus E-stop input is a monitor; Windows and USB are not the safety circuit.
